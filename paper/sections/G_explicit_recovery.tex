\subsection{The alphabet-independent frame gap}
For unit-modulus matrices whose nonzero Gram entries have modulus at least $a$, a non-Butson matrix has $Q\ge a^2\lceil n/2\rceil$. If a support is isolate-free, count edges. Otherwise orient the core orders $v\ge v'$ and apply \cref{lem:frame-new} at a minimum-degree vertex: $n\le dv'\le2mv'/v\le2m$.
For sixth roots, Gram norms are integers. At norm-obstructed odd orders the lowest energy $(n+1)/2$ is impossible by the proof of \cref{thm:odd-gap} with scale one. The isolate-free determinant is $n(n^2-2)(n^2-1)^{(n-3)/2}$; the regular-core determinant is $n^{n-v/d}(n^2-d^2)^{v/(2d)}$. Each has odd inert-prime valuation. Hence $Q\ge(n+3)/2$. The further order-fifteen improvement is recorded in \cref{app:sixth-improvement}.
